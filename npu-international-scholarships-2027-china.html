About the scholarships

Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU) in Xi’an offers two genuine international scholarship routes for its 2027 admission season — officially published for 2027 on 13 October 2026: the NPU President Scholarship and the Chinese Government Scholarship (University Program, Type B). The 2026 cycles are closed. (An aggregator page mashes these together as one scheme — they are separate routes with separate deadlines, described accurately below.)

NPU President Scholarship 2027 — four tiers

  • Elite: tuition waiver + accommodation waiver + living allowance — RMB 1,500/month × 10 months (UG), RMB 2,000 × 10 (master’s), RMB 2,500 × 10 (PhD).
  • First level: full tuition waiver.
  • Second level: 50% tuition waiver.
  • Third level: 25% tuition waiver.
First year only: the President Scholarship is a freshman award covering the first academic year, with an annual comprehensive review — it can be downgraded or terminated, and there is no upward adjustment.

CSC University Program (Type B) 2027 at NPU

The CSC route is open to master’s and PhD applicants only, with NPU’s agency number 10699. Applications must go through NPU first — other channels are not accepted. Two CSC batches run: 1 November – 31 December 2026 (17:00 GMT+8) and 1 January – 28 February 2027 (17:00 GMT+8).

Exact CSC stipend figures (tuition, accommodation, the national 3,000–3,500 CNY/month rates, insurance) were not confirmed on NPU’s official CSC page in this check — they are omitted here rather than filled in from aggregators. The 600 RMB application fee seen on some sites is likewise not on the official pages.

Eligibility

  • Non-Chinese citizen with a valid passport, in good health (special nationality rules apply for former Chinese citizens).
  • Undergraduate: high-school diploma, under 23 before 1 September 2027, with CSCA results (SAT, A-Level or IB accepted as supplementary).
  • Master’s: bachelor’s degree, under 30, with an average score of 75% or above.
  • PhD: master’s degree, under 35.
  • Chinese or English proficiency meeting NPU’s admission criteria.
  • All majors offered to international students are covered.

Documents required

The document list is not published on the President Scholarship page itself — applicants must pull the checklist from NPU’s official 2027 application guides (bachelor’s guide and master’s/PhD guide, linked in the official sources). Contacts: admission-ug@nwpu.edu.cn (undergraduate), admission-pg@nwpu.edu.cn (postgraduate), tel 86-29-88494381.

How to apply

  1. Apply through the NPU Online Application System in your batch — undergraduate batches: 1 Nov – 31 Dec 2026, 1 Jan – 28 Feb 2027, 1 Mar – 15 Apr 2027, 16 Apr – 20 May 2027 (September intake only); postgraduate batches: 1 Nov – 31 Dec 2026 (17:00), 1 Jan – 28 Feb 2027, 1 Mar – 31 Mar 2027, 1 Apr – 30 Apr 2027. Submissions after 30 April 2027 17:00 are automatically void.
  2. For the CSC route: apply through NPU first (agency 10699, Type B) within the CSC batch windows above.
  3. Prepare documents per the official 2027 guides linked on NPU’s page — do not rely on a generic aggregator checklist.
  4. Apply as early as possible: the President Scholarship is a freshman award and later batches compete for fewer remaining places.

FAQs

What are the NPU International Scholarships 2027?

Two separate genuine routes at Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi’an, for the officially announced 2027 admission season: the tiered NPU President Scholarship and the Chinese Government Scholarship (University Program, Type B).

How much does the President Scholarship cover?

The Elite tier waives tuition and accommodation and adds a living allowance (RMB 1,500/month × 10 for undergraduates, 2,000 for master’s, 2,500 for PhD). First level waives full tuition; second level 50%; third level 25%. It covers the first year only, subject to annual review.

When is the deadline?

Applications run in four batches from 1 November 2026. The final deadlines are 30 April 2027 17:00 (postgraduate) and 20 May 2027 (undergraduate). The two CSC batches close 31 December 2026 and 28 February 2027.

Who can apply?

Non-Chinese citizens: undergraduates under 23 with a high-school diploma, master’s applicants under 30 (average ≥75%), and PhD applicants under 35 — meeting NPU’s Chinese/English proficiency requirements.

How do I apply for the CSC route?

Through NPU first (agency number 10699, Type B) — other channels are not accepted. Complete the NPU online application within the CSC batch windows.
