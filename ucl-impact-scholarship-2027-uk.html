About the scholarship

University College London (UCL) offers the IMPACT Scholarship for its online Master of Public Health (MPH). It is a small, targeted award: two bursaries for the 2027/28 intake, each worth up to £20,500, applied first to the MPH tuition fees (pro rata for part-time or modular-flexible study). What makes it different from most scholarships is the selection basis: it is for students with lived experience of inequality — the official page names income, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, carer responsibilities, adverse childhood experiences, social exclusion and disability as examples, and stresses the list is not exhaustive. Academic merit matters through the MPH admissions criteria, but the scholarship itself is judged on your story and your purpose.

The timeline is generous: applications open 1 March 2027 and close on 30 June 2027 at 11:59pm BST, with results in early August 2027. Two independent assessors score each application, and the two highest-ranked applicants are awarded. If you find a 30 June 2026 deadline on an aggregator page, ignore it — that was the 2026/27 cycle, and the official UCL page has moved on to 2027/28.

Eligibility

  • Have lived experience of inequality — for example income, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, carer responsibilities, adverse childhood experiences, social exclusion or disability (the official list is non-exhaustive).
  • Meet the admissions criteria for the UCL online Master of Public Health (MPH).
  • Have submitted an admissions application for the MPH starting in 2027/28.
  • Home and overseas fee status are both eligible.
  • Full-time, part-time and modular-flexible study are all eligible.

Benefits

  • Up to £20,500 per recipient, applied first to tuition fees of the UCL online Public Health MPH.
  • Two bursaries available for 2027/28.
  • Pro rata support for part-time or modular-flexible study.
  • A fully online MPH — study from anywhere.

Documents to prepare

  • Your MPH admissions application for 2027/28 (submitted first).
  • The IMPACT Scholarship online application (from 1 March 2027).
  • A personal statement covering: (a) how your lived experience of inequality shaped your drive to study Public Health; (b) why you need the scholarship and how it will help you personally; (c) how you will use the MPH and the scholarship to make a difference to population health.
  • Note: the official page lists no document checklist beyond the online application with a personal statement. Aggregator lists (degree certificate, medical certificate, CV and so on) are generic template filler, not from UCL.

How to apply

  1. Apply for admission to the UCL online Public Health MPH for 2027/28.
  2. From 1 March 2027, complete the IMPACT Scholarship online application on the official UCL page.
  3. Write your personal statement covering the three points above — lived experience, need, and the difference you will make.
  4. Submit before 30 June 2027, 11:59pm BST.
  5. Two independent assessors score each application; the two highest-ranked applicants are awarded, with results in early August 2027.
