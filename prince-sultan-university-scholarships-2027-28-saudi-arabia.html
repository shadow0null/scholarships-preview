About the scholarship

Prince Sultan University (PSU) in Riyadh — Saudi Arabia’s first private university, founded in 1999 — runs one of the kingdom’s most generous institutional scholarship programmes. Each year it offers tiered tuition waivers of 25%, 50% or 100% to attract outstanding new students, Saudi and international alike, across its bachelor’s, master’s and PhD programmes in business, law, computer science, engineering, architecture and more.

The university’s own July 2026 announcement confirms that distinguished students can receive an academic scholarship of up to 100% along with a monthly stipend of 1,500 SAR. Because the published package centres on tuition — with the stipend reserved for the top tier — this article markets it honestly as partially funded, not as a full ride.

The 2026-27 scholarship window (applications 1–15 July 2026, results 17 July 2026) is now closed, and the 2027-28 call has not been published yet. The guidance below is built on the verified, recurring structure of the scheme.

Eligibility

  • Be a Saudi or international student with an outstanding academic record — selection is competitive and merit-based.
  • First secure admission to Prince Sultan University. Per the university’s scholarship FAQ, students cannot apply for the scholarship until they are admitted and hold a 9-digit university ID.
  • Hold a high-school diploma or equivalent for bachelor’s programmes, or a bachelor’s degree for graduate programmes.
  • Demonstrate English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS scores are part of the file).
  • Commit to full-time study and meet the scholarship’s continuity terms — the university holds you accountable for your cumulative GPA, including the preparatory year.

Benefits

  • 25%, 50% or 100% tuition fee waiver, depending on merit tier.
  • Monthly stipend of 1,500 SAR for distinguished 100% scholarship holders, per the university’s announcement.
  • Renewable each year on academic performance (cumulative GPA terms).
  • Access to PSU’s modern Riyadh campus, research facilities and career services.

Documents required

  • Completed online scholarship application (submitted with your 9-digit PSU student ID).
  • Academic transcripts and degree/diploma certificates.
  • English proficiency scores (TOEFL/IELTS).
  • Two recommendation letters from teachers or professors.
  • A personal statement or motivation letter.
  • Passport or national ID and recent passport-size photographs.

How to apply

  1. Get admitted to PSU firstApply for admission to your chosen programme through the university’s electronic portal and receive your 9-digit student ID — no scholarship application is possible before this.
  2. Register on the scholarship portalCreate your account at info.psu.edu.sa/MyScholarShip/ and complete the separate scholarship application form.
  3. Upload your documentsAttach transcripts, English scores, recommendation letters, personal statement and ID. Double-check that every file is complete and correct.
  4. Submit before the window closesWatch for the 2027-28 announcement — the 2026-27 window ran only from 1 to about 15 July 2026, so the window is short.
  5. Await the resultSelections are announced by email (the 2026-27 results came out on 17 July 2026). If selected, you receive an offer letter.

FAQs

Is the Prince Sultan University scholarship fully funded?

Not exactly. It is a tiered tuition-waiver scheme (25%, 50% or 100%), with a 1,500 SAR monthly stipend for distinguished 100% scholars. Trackers that promise free accommodation, insurance and airfare are not backed by the university’s own publications, so this article does not repeat those claims.

Can international students apply?

Yes — the scholarships are explicitly open to both Saudi and non-Saudi students, male and female.

Do I apply for admission and the scholarship together?

No. They are two separate applications. You must first be admitted and receive your 9-digit student ID, then register for the scholarship on the scholarship portal. There is no automatic registration.

When will the 2027-28 call open?

Not yet announced. The 2026-27 window opened 1 July 2026 and closed around 15 July 2026. Expect a similar short mid-year window, but confirm the dates on psu.edu.sa once published.

What GPA do I need to keep the scholarship?

The university’s continuity terms hold you to your cumulative GPA — including the preparatory year. The exact threshold is in your scholarship approval form.

Which programmes are covered?

Most PSU degree programmes, including Finance, Accounting, Marketing, Law, Information Systems, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Architecture and Interior Design — note that some programmes are gender-specific in their intake.
