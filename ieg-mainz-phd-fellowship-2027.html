About the fellowship

The Leibniz Institute of European History (IEG) in Mainz awards residential fellowships to doctoral researchers working on European history — from the early modern period to contemporary history — and historically oriented disciplines such as religious history, intellectual history, environmental history, and digital humanities. The fellowship supports the writing and completion stage of the dissertation: a 6- to 12-month stay in Mainz to finish your thesis with mentoring, library access, and a research community around you.

What it is not: the fellowship does not fund the start of a research project, language courses, or the revision of a finished dissertation into a book. Your source research should already be well advanced.

Eligibility

  • Enrolled PhD researcher (international or German) working on European history or a historically oriented discipline.
  • Remain enrolled and supervised at your home university during the stay — IEG does not award the degree.
  • Normally no more than three years into the dissertation by the deadline (justified exceptions possible).
  • A mature dissertation concept that would benefit from a 6–12 month writing period.
  • Proficiency in English expected.

Benefits

  • Monthly stipend of €1,650.
  • Family allowance: €250 (partner), €400 (first child), €100 (each additional child).
  • Mentoring from IEG researchers, plus institute workspace and library.
  • Affordable room in the institute's residence, subject to availability.
  • Fellows live in Mainz full-time, join weekly colloquia, and present their work at least once.

Documents required

  1. Completed application form
  2. Valid passport
  3. Academic transcript + degree certificate
  4. Academic CV (and publication list, if any)
  5. Dissertation table of contents
  6. Project description (max 15,000 characters, excl. spaces and footnotes)
  7. Detailed work plan and timeline for the stay
  8. Language certificates, where applicable

How to apply

  1. Go to the IEG application portalbewerbung.ieg-mainz.de — doctoral fellowships section.
  2. Prepare your project descriptionExplain the current stage of your dissertation and how the Mainz stay will complete it.
  3. Submit before 15 February 2027The call typically opens in December; confirm the exact date on the portal.

FAQs

How much is the stipend?

€1,650 per month, plus family allowances where applicable.

Can I work alongside the fellowship?

No — paid employment and simultaneous financial support are not permitted during the funded period.

Does IEG award my PhD?

No — you stay enrolled and supervised at your home university; IEG funds the writing stay.

Is accommodation free?

No — fellows can apply for an affordable room in the institute's residence, subject to availability.
