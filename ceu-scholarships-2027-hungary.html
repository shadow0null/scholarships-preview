About the scholarship

Central European University (CEU) — an international graduate university with campuses in Vienna and Budapest — awards merit-based scholarships across its Bachelor’s, Master’s and PhD programmes. Over 80% of CEU students receive some form of financial aid. Awards combine tuition coverage (up to 100%) with monthly stipends and health insurance.

Round 1 closed 15 October 2026. The next window is Round 2, closing 2 February 2027 (financial aid open in Round 2; applicants from visa-sensitive countries should check eligibility for later rounds).

Eligibility

  • Applying to a CEU Bachelor’s, Master’s or PhD programme.
  • Strong academic record — scholarships are awarded on merit (and need, for some tiers).
  • English proficiency (exemptions for prior English-taught degrees from listed countries).
  • PhD: master’s degree required.
  • All nationalities welcome.

Benefits

  • Tuition: up to 100% coverage.
  • Master’s stipends: €1,000/month (Excellence), €750/month (Distinction), €500/month (Master’s Scholarship).
  • Doctoral fellowship: monthly stipend plus health insurance.
  • Health insurance and housing/living support.

Documents required

  1. Online application form
  2. Degree certificates + transcripts
  3. CV
  4. Statement of purpose
  5. Recommendation letters
  6. Research proposal (PhD / some master’s)
  7. English proficiency proof

How to apply

  1. Create an account on CEU’s online application portalPick your programme and check its requirements.
  2. Submit the application + documentsPay the €30 application fee.
  3. Meet the 2 February 2027 Round 2 deadlineScholarship consideration is automatic with admission.

FAQs

What is the next deadline?

Round 2 closes 2 February 2027. Round 1 (15 Oct 2026) has closed.

How much can I get?

Up to 100% tuition plus €500–€1,000/month for master’s students; doctoral fellowships for PhD students.

Are courses in English?

Yes — all CEU programmes are taught in English.

Is there a separate scholarship form?

No — you are considered for financial aid through the admission application.
