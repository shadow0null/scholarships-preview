How it works

How a listing gets on this page

1

Checked against the source

Each listing is checked against the provider's own notification, portal or press release before publication. The verification date shown on every page is that listing's own date — never a site-wide claim.

2

Official or mentioned by

A link is called “Official source” only when it goes to the provider or a government portal. Aggregator pages (Buddy4Study, Bright Scholarship, Opportunity Desk and others) are labelled “Mentioned by”, never “Official source”.

3

Apply on the official site

Applications always happen on the provider's website — never here. Deadlines are recomputed every day from the listing's own date, so a closed scheme never shows as open.

Not a government website

StudyDesk is an independent education guide — not a government website and not affiliated with any provider listed here. Always confirm details on the official portal before applying.
