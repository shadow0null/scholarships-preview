About the scholarship

The University of Calgary International Entrance Scholarship is the university’s flagship entrance award for international undergraduates: CAD 20,000, offered to just 2 students each year entering first year of any undergraduate degree. It is renewable in years two, three and four — worth up to CAD 80,000 in total — as long as you keep a 2.60 GPA over at least 24.00 units in the previous fall and winter terms and remain an international fee-paying student.

Unlike many automatic entrance awards, this one needs a separate application through UCalgary’s High School Prestige Awards competition — and that competition’s deadline is 1 December 2026 for fall 2027 entry. With only two awards on offer, apply early and treat the Prestige Awards form as seriously as your admission application.

Eligibility

  • Be an international student — not a Canadian citizen or permanent resident — required to pay international tuition fees.
  • Be entering first year of any undergraduate degree at the University of Calgary in the upcoming fall term (fall 2027).
  • Have satisfied the university’s English Language Proficiency requirement.
  • Show strong academic merit — the official award page states merit as the criterion, with no fixed admission-average cutoff published.
  • To renew: a GPA of 2.60+ over at least 24.00 units in the previous fall and winter terms, remaining enrolled and paying international fees.

Benefits

  • CAD 20,000 in the first year.
  • Renewable for years 2–4 (up to CAD 80,000 total) on a 2.60 GPA over 24.00 units per year.
  • Open to any undergraduate faculty — business, engineering, science, arts and more.
  • Study at one of Canada’s leading research universities in Calgary, Alberta.

Documents required

  • Undergraduate admission application with official high-school transcripts and certificates.
  • Prestige Awards application completed in the My UCalgary Portal (essay/personal-statement responses on leadership and goals, as prompted).
  • English proficiency proof (IELTS/TOEFL or equivalent) if required for your admission stream.
  • All admission documents uploaded by 15 December 2026 (check your Applicant Portal to-do list).

FAQs

Is the CAD 20,000 per year or one-time?

It is CAD 20,000 for the first year and renewable in years two, three and four — up to CAD 80,000 total — provided you keep a 2.60 GPA over at least 24.00 units each year and remain an international fee-paying student.

Do I need a separate application?

Yes. Admission alone is not enough — you must complete the High School Prestige Awards application in the My UCalgary Portal by 1 December 2026.

How many awards are available?

Only 2 per year, which makes this one of UCalgary’s most competitive international awards.

Is there a fixed grade cutoff?

The official award page states “academic merit” without publishing a numeric admission average. Apply with the strongest grades you have.

What is the difference from the President’s Admission Scholarship?

That is a separate, automatic award: CAD 5,000, non-renewable, based on a 95%+ final admission average, with no application. This International Entrance Scholarship is larger, renewable, competitive (2 awards), and needs the Prestige Awards application.

When will I hear back?

Notifications go out in March/April, after the December deadlines.
