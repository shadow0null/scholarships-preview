About the scholarship

The Vidyasirimedhi Institute of Science and Technology (VISTEC) in Rayong, Thailand — the country’s graduate research institute focused on science and technology — offers a fully funded scholarship for its MS and PhD programmes, worth up to THB 5 million per awardee with no post-graduation obligation.

The queue’s “2026” framing is stale: the 2026 intake (deadline 15 February 2026) is closed. The 2027 intake is open right now — applications opened 1 October 2026 on VISTEC’s official admission portal and close on 15 February 2027.

Eligibility

  • Open to all nationalities — Thai and international applicants.
  • Master’s applicants: a bachelor’s degree. PhD applicants: a master’s degree.
  • The degree must be in a field relevant to one of VISTEC’s four schools: Molecular Science and Engineering (MSE), Energy Science and Engineering (ESE), Biomolecular Science and Engineering (BSE), or Information Science and Technology (IST).
  • English test score required before the interview — TOEFL, IELTS or CU-TEP (equivalent or other scores accepted temporarily).

Benefits

  • University fees waived and on-campus housing/dormitory provided.
  • Monthly stipend up to THB 20,000.
  • Research budget support up to THB 500,000 and study/equipment support up to THB 30,000.
  • PhD enrollees: up to THB 1 million for up to one year of research abroad.
  • High-performing PhD students may additionally apply for the Royal Srimedhi Scholarship for up to 2 years abroad.

Documents required

  • CV and transcript.
  • Statement of purpose — the official form is downloadable during the application and can be submitted up to the interview day.
  • Two letters of recommendation (same late-submission allowance).
  • Official English test score (TOEFL/IELTS/CU-TEP) before the interview.
  • Optional: publications, awards & honours, portfolio.
  • Contact for admissions questions: admission@vistec.ac.th, +66 33 01 4116.

How to apply

  1. Explore programmesRead the schools and programmes on the official VISTEC admission portal.
  2. Create an accountRegister on admission.vistec.ac.th — a maximum of 2 application forms per applicant is allowed.
  3. Complete the form and upload documentsFill in the application, upload your documents, and submit the statement of purpose (which may be finalised up to the interview day).
  4. Shortlisting and examIf shortlisted, you are notified on 22 February 2027 and attend the interview and written exam on 27 February 2027; results are announced on 31 March 2027.

FAQs

Is the VISTEC scholarship fully funded?

Yes — the official admission page describes a full scholarship worth up to THB 5 million: fees waived, dormitory, up to THB 20,000/month stipend, research and equipment budgets, and no post-graduation obligation.

Who can apply?

All nationalities. Master’s applicants need a bachelor’s degree and PhD applicants need a master’s, in a field relevant to one of VISTEC’s four schools (MSE, ESE, BSE, IST).

Do I need an English test score?

Yes — an official English test score (TOEFL, IELTS or CU-TEP) is required before the interview.

Can I submit more than one application?

A maximum of 2 application forms per applicant is allowed on the admission portal.

Is there an application fee?

One third-party listing mentions a USD 20 fee for international applicants, but this is not confirmed by a second source — treat it as unverified and check the official portal before paying anything.
