About the scholarship

JUSTCOOL: PhD is a joint doctoral programme of three leading Shanghai universities — Fudan University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Tongji University — offering international students direct PhD entry straight after a bachelor’s degree (no master’s required). The five-year, English-taught PhD covers Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology, with a fellowship, tuition waiver, free accommodation and medical insurance.

Eligibility

  • Non-Chinese citizen holding (or about to hold) a bachelor’s degree.
  • Top 20% of class academically; graduation date must fit the programme rules.
  • Applying for Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry or Biology.
  • English proficiency: English-medium education or an accepted test (IELTS not strictly compulsory).
  • Prepared to sit the written examinations (12 Dec 2026) and interviews.

Benefits

  • PhD fellowship plus research/teaching assistantship support (exact stipend not stated on the official site).
  • Tuition waiver and free university accommodation.
  • Medical insurance coverage.
  • Cross-university course access across the three partners.

Documents required

  1. Online application on the JUSTCOOL portal
  2. Bachelor’s degree certificate + transcripts
  3. CV
  4. Personal statement / study plan
  5. English proficiency proof
  6. Passport copy
  7. Recommendation letters

How to apply

  1. Apply on the official portalapply.justcoolphd.cn — choose your subject first (university choice comes after interview).
  2. Submit before 14 Nov 2026, 24:00 Beijing timeApplication window: 28 Sep – 14 Nov 2026.
  3. Written exam 12 Dec 2026, interviews January 2027Admission notices in February 2027; enrolment spring/autumn 2027.
Stipend amount not published: the official site confirms a fellowship and assistantship but does not state the monthly figure — verify your award letter before accepting.

FAQs

Do I need a master’s degree?

No — JUSTCOOL admits directly after a bachelor’s degree.

Which universities take part?

Fudan University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Tongji University.

What subjects are offered?

Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

Is there an entrance exam?

Yes — written examinations on 12 December 2026, then interviews in January 2027.
