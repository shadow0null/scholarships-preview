About the scholarship

The STIBET Scholarship at Anhalt University of Applied Sciences (Hochschule Anhalt) is a short-term grant funded by the DAAD and the Federal Foreign Office, designed to help international students in the final stage of their studies. It pays €400 a month for three months — €1,200 in total — so that a student can focus on finishing a thesis, or to recognise outstanding voluntary work in the university community.

The important thing to understand is what this grant is not. It is not a degree scholarship and it is not open to new applicants: only international students already enrolled full-time at Anhalt can apply, in one of two categories. The Graduation Scholarship supports students close to finishing, who will submit their thesis within a semester. The Scholarship for Particularly Committed Students rewards students who have actively volunteered in international or social activities while keeping good grades. If you are already at Anhalt and in your final stage, €1,200 of breathing room is real money — but do not plan a degree around it.

Eligibility

  • Be an international student (non-German nationality) enrolled full-time in a Bachelor’s or Master’s programme at Anhalt University of Applied Sciences.
  • Have good or very good academic results.
  • For the Graduation Scholarship: be in the final stage of your degree, have completed most required credits, plan to submit your thesis within one semester, and live in Germany during the funding period.
  • For the Scholarship for Particularly Committed Students: have completed at least one semester at Anhalt and demonstrate active participation in recognised voluntary, international or social activities, with evidence.
  • Prospective students applying from outside Germany are not eligible.

Benefits

  • €400 a month for three months — €1,200 in total.
  • Financial support to focus on your final thesis or recognition for voluntary community work.
  • Short-term help with living costs during the final study period.
  • Note that this is not full funding — it does not cover tuition, travel, all living costs, health insurance or accommodation.

Documents to prepare

  • The online application form on the official scholarship webpage.
  • A motivation letter (maximum 1,800 characters, one A4 page).
  • Your academic transcript (His-Qis extract).
  • A tabular CV (maximum 2 pages).
  • A copy of your passport or identity card.
  • A reference letter from a professor at Anhalt, sent directly to stibet.scholarship@hs-anhalt.de.
  • Optional: a provisional timetable for degree completion and confirmation of registration for your final thesis or exam.

How to apply

  1. Check that you meet one of the two categories — Graduation Scholarship or Scholarship for Particularly Committed Students.
  2. Complete the online application form on the official scholarship webpage.
  3. Upload your motivation letter (maximum 1,800 characters), His-Qis transcript, tabular CV (maximum 2 pages) and passport copy.
  4. Ask a professor at Anhalt to send your reference letter directly to stibet.scholarship@hs-anhalt.de.
  5. Optionally add your provisional timetable and thesis registration confirmation.
  6. Submit all documents electronically before 15 April 2027. The university confirms receipt by email.

FAQs

Is the Anhalt STIBET Scholarship fully funded?

No. It is a short-term grant of €400 a month for three months, €1,200 in total. It does not cover tuition, travel, all living costs, health insurance or accommodation.

Can students outside Germany apply?

No. The scholarship is only for international students already enrolled full-time at Anhalt University of Applied Sciences. Prospective students applying for admission from outside Germany are not eligible.

What is the deadline for the Anhalt STIBET Scholarships 2027?

15 April 2027. Late applications cannot be considered.

What are the two STIBET categories?

The Graduation Scholarship, for students in the final stage of their degree who will submit their thesis within one semester; and the Scholarship for Particularly Committed Students, for students with a proven record of voluntary international or social activities at Anhalt.

What documents do I need?

A motivation letter (maximum 1,800 characters), your His-Qis academic transcript, a tabular CV (maximum 2 pages), a copy of your passport or identity card, and a reference letter from an Anhalt professor sent directly to stibet.scholarship@hs-anhalt.de.

How much is the STIBET grant?
