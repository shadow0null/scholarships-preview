Privacy

StudyDesk Scholarships is a free listing guide. This page states plainly what we store, what we do not store, and where applications actually happen.

No account

There is no account, no sign-in, and no profile on this site. You can browse every listing and save scholarships without giving us your name, email, or phone number.

Saved scholarships stay on your device

When you save a scholarship to My plan, it is stored in your browser's localStorage on your own device, together with your “hide WhatsApp” preference where that preference applies. Nothing is uploaded to our server, nothing is synced to another device, and we cannot see what you saved. Clearing your browser's site data removes your saved list.

Applications are never submitted here

Applications always happen on the provider's own website or portal — never on StudyDesk Scholarships. We do not collect application forms, documents, or personal details for any scholarship.

Advertising

Ads may be served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads on this site. We do not control Google's cookies; you can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings and by clearing cookies in your browser.

Not a government website

StudyDesk is an independent education guide — not a government website and not affiliated with any provider listed here. Always confirm details on the official portal before applying.
