About the scholarships

Dongguan University of Technology (DGUT) is a public university in Dongguan, Guangdong Province, China, and its scholarships for international master’s students come in two official parts. The first is the Guangdong Provincial Government Scholarship for international postgraduates: RMB 20,000, one-off, for students with a bachelor’s degree who are 40 or under. The second is the DGUT Scholarship for international students, which pays 75%, 50% or 25% of the year’s tuition — First, Second and Third Prize, with quotas of 10%, 20% and 30% of recipients. Tuition itself is RMB 20,000 per academic year for postgraduate courses, and the registration fee is RMB 450 (non-refundable).

That is the official picture, from DGUT’s own 2026 International Student Enrollment Guide. It is deliberately narrower than the “fully funded” package quoted on aggregator sites — a freshman scholarship of RMB 20,000 a year, a living allowance of RMB 10,000 a year, accommodation of RMB 1,500–3,000 a year and medical insurance of about RMB 800 a year — which does not appear in the official guide at all. It may reflect a newer cycle, but it is not officially confirmed, so this article does not present it as the award. The master’s programmes are three years, full-time, starting each September, taught in Chinese, and cover engineering and related fields: Software Engineering, Communication Engineering, Control Engineering, Optoelectronic Information Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Civil and Hydraulic Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Energy and Power Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Materials and Chemical Engineering, New-Generation Electronic Information Technology, Integrated Circuit Engineering, Biology and Medicine, Food and Nutrition, International Business, Digital Economy and Social Work.

Eligibility

  • Be a non-Chinese citizen holding a valid foreign passport, in good health.
  • Hold a bachelor’s degree equivalent to a Chinese university degree (fresh graduates may submit a pre-graduation certificate).
  • For science and engineering majors taught in Chinese: HSK Level 4 with a score of 180 or above — otherwise Chinese-language study first.
  • For the Guangdong Provincial Government Scholarship: be 40 or under and apply before 1 September each year.
  • Ethnic-Chinese applicants must meet the 4-year naturalisation / 2-year residence rule.

Benefits

  • Guangdong Provincial Government Scholarship: RMB 20,000, one-off, for international postgraduates.
  • DGUT Scholarship: 75% / 50% / 25% of the year’s tuition (First / Second / Third Prize).
  • Tuition: RMB 20,000 per academic year for postgraduate courses; registration fee RMB 450 (non-refundable).
  • Not covered: airfare, daily meals, visa fees, residence permit fees and personal expenses.
  • Not officially confirmed: the aggregator “freshman package” (RMB 20,000 a year + RMB 10,000 living allowance + accommodation + medical insurance) — do not budget around it.

Documents to prepare

  • A valid passport (plus the current visa/residence permit page if already in mainland China).
  • A bachelor’s degree certificate (a pre-graduation certificate is acceptable for fresh graduates).
  • A complete undergraduate transcript.
  • A language proficiency certificate (HSK for Chinese-taught majors).
  • Reference letters signed by two professors or associate professors.
  • A study and research plan in China.
  • Proof of financial status.
  • A health examination report.
  • A letter of commitment and other supporting materials as required.
  • Documents must be in Chinese or English (notarised translations otherwise).

How to apply

  1. Prepare the application form and supporting documents listed above.
  2. Email them to gjc@dgut.edu.cn, or apply through the official WeChat student consultation account “studyatDGUT”.
  3. Go through document verification and interview.
  4. Receive the admission letter and visa documents (JW202).
  5. Apply for a student visa.
  6. Note the application fee of about RMB 450 after pre-admission (per Bright; the official guide’s registration fee is RMB 450).

FAQs

What is the deadline for DGUT Scholarships 2027?

It has not been announced by DGUT for the 2027 cycle. Aggregators list 30 June 2027, but ScholarshipsAds itself states the 2027 deadline has not been announced and no 2027 DGUT admission notice was found. Do not plan around 30 June 2027; watch the official DGUT channels.

Is the DGUT scholarship fully funded?

Not on the official evidence. The official award is a Guangdong provincial scholarship of RMB 20,000 (one-off) plus a DGUT tuition scholarship of 75%, 50% or 25%. The “fully funded” freshman package quoted by aggregators (RMB 20,000 a year plus a living allowance and accommodation) is not in the official 2026 guide.

How long is the master’s at DGUT?

Three years, full-time, starting each September and taught in Chinese — aggregators saying two years are wrong.

What Chinese level do I need?

For science and engineering majors taught in Chinese, HSK Level 4 with a score of 180 or above; otherwise you study Chinese first.

How do I apply to DGUT?

Email the completed application form and supporting documents to gjc@dgut.edu.cn, or apply through the official WeChat account “studyatDGUT”. Then comes document verification and an interview, then the admission letter and JW202 visa documents.

Is DGUT the same as GDUT?

No. DGUT is Dongguan University of Technology; GDUT is Guangdong University of Technology — a different university. Several searches mix the two up, so check the domain (dgut.edu.cn) before you apply.
