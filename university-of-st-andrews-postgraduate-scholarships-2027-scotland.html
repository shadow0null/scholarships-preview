The University of St Andrews Postgraduate Scholarships 2027 are really two routes under one umbrella: the St Leonard’s Master’s Scholarship for international students, which can pay anything from a quarter of your tuition up to full tuition plus an £18,000 living allowance, and the St Leonard’s College World-Leading Doctoral Scholarships, up to six fully funded PhD awards. The doctoral route closes on 20 January 2027 and the master’s route on 3 February 2027, and a single scholarship application puts you in the running for many awards at once.

Deadline: 20 Jan 2027 Closes 20 Jan 2027 Updated 16 Oct 2026 By University of St Andrews
Award
Master’s route (St Leonard’s Master’s Scholarship, international students): tiered awards — Global Widening Participation covers full tuition plus £18,000 towards living costs; Leadership covers 75% of tuition plus up to £2,000 for approved career activities; Excellence covers 25% or 50% of tuition. Doctoral route (World-Leading Doctoral Scholarships): up to 6 awards covering full tuition plus an annual stipend (the 2026/27 reference stipend quoted in secondary summaries is £20,467 — confirm the 2027/28 figure on st-andrews.ac.uk). Master’s funding lasts one year; for eligible longer research programmes it covers the first year only.
Eligible
Master’s: students with Overseas fee status applying for eligible full-time one-year taught master’s degrees, one-year research master’s, and some two-year research master’s with a taught first year, starting in September 2027 (January 2028, or August 2027 for the Master of Public Policy, where applicable). Doctoral: PhD applicants in any field, with no topic limits across the qualifying schools; you must not already hold a doctorate or be enrolled in a doctoral programme elsewhere. Assessment is on academic excellence, wider achievement, leadership and financial need — not on financial need alone.
How
Apply for your St Andrews course first, then submit one scholarship application through the university’s My Application portal. That single application automatically considers you for the other awards inside the St Leonard’s scheme, and for any further awards added to the scheme after you apply.
Deadline
Doctoral route: 20 January 2027. Master’s route (international): 3 February 2027. The master’s application period opened 17 August 2026; results are expected in the week beginning 22 March 2027.
Fine print
The largest master’s award also reserves a standard room for you, but the room is not free — you pay for accommodation from your living-cost allowance or other funds. Doctoral applicants whose proposed research advances one of the university’s world-leading strategic topics are the ones considered for the World-Leading Doctoral Scholarships.

About the scholarships

The University of St Andrews, Scotland’s oldest university, runs its most prestigious postgraduate funding through St Leonard’s College, its postgraduate college. For 2027 entry the scheme is deliberately designed as one door into many rooms: instead of applying separately to a dozen small funds, you submit a single scholarship application and the panel considers you for every award in the scheme you qualify for — including named awards such as the R&A Ransome Scholarship and the Valerie Graeme Smith Scholarship, with more added during the cycle.

That matters because the two routes are genuinely different in scale. The master’s route is a tiered fee-waiver scheme: most winners get a slice of their tuition, while the top Global Widening Participation tier is effectively a full ride plus a living allowance. The doctoral route is much smaller and much deeper — up to six fully funded PhD places a year, aimed at research the university classes as world-leading.

Eligibility

  • Master’s route: hold Overseas fee status and an application (or offer) for an eligible full-time taught or research master’s starting in 2027 — one-year taught degrees, one-year research degrees, and some two-year research degrees with a taught first year. Only the first year of a two-year research master’s is funded.
  • Doctoral route: be applying for a PhD at St Andrews; you must not already hold a doctorate or be enrolled in a doctoral programme elsewhere. There are no topic limits across the qualifying schools.
  • Meet at least one of the scheme’s criteria — you do not need to meet all of them. The panel weighs academic excellence, wider achievement, leadership, financial need, disability, minority background and other contextual factors.
  • For the doctoral route, research that advances one of the university’s world-leading strategic topics is what the World-Leading awards target.
  • Course admission comes first: you apply for the degree, then for the scholarship, through the same My Application portal.

Benefits

  • Global Widening Participation (master’s): full tuition fees plus £18,000 towards living costs, plus a reserved standard room (paid for by you, not free).
  • Leadership (master’s): 75% of tuition fees plus up to £2,000 for approved career-development activities.
  • Excellence (master’s): 25% or 50% of tuition fees.
  • World-Leading Doctoral: up to 6 awards covering full tuition plus an annual stipend.
  • One application covers many named awards inside the scheme — and any awards added after you apply.

Documents to prepare

  • Your St Andrews course application (submitted first) and any offer you hold.
  • Degree certificate and academic transcripts.
  • A CV or résumé and a cover letter / personal statement setting out your achievements, leadership and any financial need.
  • A research proposal where your school asks for one for course admission; where it does not, an appropriate personal statement may be used instead.
  • A valid passport and any other supporting documents the scholarship form requests.

How to apply

  1. Apply for an eligible St Andrews postgraduate course for 2027 entry.
  2. Open the St Leonard’s scholarship application in the My Application portal (the master’s window opened 17 August 2026).
  3. Complete the single scholarship application — it automatically considers you for the other awards in the scheme.
  4. Submit by 20 January 2027 (doctoral) or 3 February 2027 (master’s, international students).
  5. Expect results in the week beginning 22 March 2027 for the master’s route (doctoral results are also expected in late March 2027).

FAQs

Is there one application for all St Andrews postgraduate scholarships?

For the St Leonard’s scheme, yes. You submit one scholarship application through My Application and are automatically considered for the other awards in the scheme, including any added after you apply.

What is the biggest master’s award?

The Global Widening Participation award: full tuition fees plus £18,000 towards living costs. Note the reserved standard room that comes with it is not free — you pay for it from your allowance or other funds.

Can a two-year research master’s be fully funded?

No. Only the first year of an eligible two-year research master’s is funded under the master’s route.

Do I need a separate application for the doctoral scholarships?

The doctoral route has its own deadline (20 January 2027) and is aimed at research advancing the university’s world-leading strategic topics. Apply for your PhD first, then follow the doctoral scholarship route on st-andrews.ac.uk.

When will I hear back?

Master’s results are expected in the week beginning 22 March 2027; doctoral results are also expected in late March 2027.
