Fine print
Do not confuse this award with two separate January 2027 awards on the same official site. The Postgraduate Sanctuary Scholarship is genuinely fully funded — a full tuition waiver plus £1,000 a month for up to 12 months, 2 awards — but only for asylum seekers resident in the UK, with a deadline of 2 November 2026, 5pm. The Home Postgraduate Part Fee Scholarship is £2,000 for Home fee students only, also closing 2 November 2026, 5pm. Neither is the award this article covers. No taught-postgraduate Vice-Chancellor’s Scholarship was found on Westminster’s site, so none is presented here. One listing claims applicants "will be informed of the outcome by 21 November 2026" — impossible, since that precedes the 30 November deadline, and it is not used here.

About the award

The International Postgraduate Excellence Award – January 2027 is the University of Westminster’s main partial scholarship for international students starting a taught Master’s in January 2027. It is awarded on academic excellence: the university selects the strongest applicants from those who hold an offer for an eligible full-time Master’s and apply for the award before the deadline.

What it pays is straightforward and modest: £3,000 off tuition for a one-year full-time Master’s, or £5,000 for the two-year Master’s with Integrated Professional Year Experience. It is a tuition-fee reduction only — it cannot be put towards the £5,500 CAS deposit, and it pays nothing towards living costs, travel or a visa. If you are budgeting for a year in London, treat this as a discount on the fee, not as funding for the year.

Eligibility

  • Be classified as overseas fee status.
  • Hold an offer for a full-time Master’s starting January 2027.
  • Hold a minimum UK equivalent Good 2.1 Honours degree.
  • Be selected principally on academic excellence.
  • Not eligible: professional courses (for example RIBA Part III), doctoral programmes, online or distance learning, Postgraduate Certificates or Diplomas, or anyone eligible for another tuition discount such as an alumni or partner discount.
  • Nationality is open — the requirement is overseas fee status, not a particular country.

Documents to prepare

  • Proof of your course offer for a full-time Master’s starting January 2027.
  • Transcripts showing the equivalent of a Good 2.1 Honours degree (the official page works from the course application and its country-equivalency chart).
  • Note that the official page does not publish a full document checklist beyond the application form, the offer and the transcripts. Lists on aggregator sites (passport, motivation letter, CV, cover letter, degree certificates, language proficiency) were not confirmed on the official page and are not presented here as official requirements.

How to apply

  1. Apply for an eligible full-time Master’s starting January 2027 at the University of Westminster and secure an offer.
  2. Open the official award page and submit the scholarship application through its "Apply now" link.
  3. Make sure your offer is in place in time to meet the scholarship deadline.
  4. Submit the scholarship application by 30 November 2026.
  5. Note that a separate scholarship application is required — the award is not automatic on offer, whatever some listings claim.

FAQs

Is the Westminster International Postgraduate Excellence Award fully funded?

No. It is a partial tuition-fee reduction of £3,000 (one-year Master’s) or £5,000 (two-year Master’s with Integrated Professional Year Experience). It does not cover living costs, travel, visa costs or the £5,500 CAS deposit.

What is the deadline for the Westminster Excellence Award 2027?

30 November 2026. The exact time of day on 30 November is not published on the official page.

Do I need to apply separately for the scholarship?

Yes. A separate scholarship application is required through the "Apply now" link on the official award page. The award is not automatic on offer, whatever some listings claim.

Can the award be used for the CAS deposit?

No. The award is applied as a tuition-fee reduction only and cannot go towards the £5,500 CAS deposit.

What is the Postgraduate Sanctuary Scholarship?

A separate, genuinely fully funded award at Westminster: a full tuition waiver plus £1,000 a month for up to 12 months, with 2 awards. It is only for asylum seekers resident in the UK, and its deadline was 2 November 2026, 5pm. It is not the award this article covers.

Who is not eligible?

Students on professional courses (for example RIBA Part III), doctoral programmes, online or distance learning, and Postgraduate Certificates or Diplomas; and anyone eligible for another tuition discount such as an alumni or partner discount.
