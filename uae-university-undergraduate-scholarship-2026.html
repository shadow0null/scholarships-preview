About the scholarship

The United Arab Emirates University (UAEU), the country’s oldest national research university based in Al Ain, offers merit-based undergraduate scholarships that discount tuition fees for high-achieving new students. Students with a Grade 12 average of 98% or above receive a 100% tuition discount, and those with 95% or above receive 75%. The scheme is open to international students, not only UAE nationals.

No fixed deadline published: UAEU’s official scholarship page does not publish a single application deadline. Admissions run in intake windows — May to July for the first semester and September to November for the second semester (the September–November window is open as of 20 October 2026). The official document checklist is also not published; apply through the UAEU admissions office and follow its instructions.

Eligibility

  • New undergraduate students at UAEU, including international applicants.
  • 100% tuition discount: Grade 12 average of 98% or above. 75% discount: 95% or above.
  • Meet UAEU’s admission requirements for the chosen college.
  • English proficiency: IELTS 7.5 or 6.5 bands (depending on college) or the equivalent TOEFL score.
  • Mathematics proficiency: SAT-MATH 730 or 660 for colleges that are not otherwise exempt.
  • Arabic proficiency is required for the College of Law.

Benefits

  • 100% or 75% tuition-fee discount, depending on the Grade 12 average.
  • Renewable: keep a GPA of 3.6–3.8 and complete 15 credit hours per term to retain the award.
Tuition only: the discount covers tuition fees. Housing and admission fees are excluded, and it is not a full-ride scholarship — plan for accommodation and living costs separately.

Documents required

The official scholarship document list is not published in the official notification — only aggregator-compiled lists exist, which this article does not present as official. Apply through UAEU’s undergraduate admissions and supply whatever the admissions office asks for.

How to apply

  1. Check your Grade 12 average against the tiers98%+ for full tuition, 95%+ for three-quarters.
  2. Meet the college’s entry testsEnglish (IELTS/TOEFL), mathematics (SAT-MATH) and Arabic (Law) where required.
  3. Apply for undergraduate admission within the intake windowMay–July (first semester) or September–November (second semester), via UAEU’s official admissions route.
  4. Ask the admissions office about the scholarship stepThe official page points applicants to the university’s scholarship information; confirm how the discount is attached to your admission.
  5. Keep your grades up to renewAim for a 3.6–3.8 GPA and 15 credit hours per term.

FAQs

How much does the UAE University scholarship cover?

A 100% tuition discount for students with a Grade 12 average of 98% or above, and 75% for 95% or above. Housing and admission fees are not covered.

Can international students apply?

Yes — UAEU’s merit scholarships are open to new undergraduate students including internationals.

What is the application deadline?

No fixed deadline is published. UAEU admits in intake windows: May to July for the first semester and September to November for the second semester — the latter is open as of 20 October 2026.

How do I keep the scholarship?

Meet the per-term renewal bar: a GPA of roughly 3.6–3.8 and 15 credit hours completed.

What entry tests are needed?

English (IELTS/TOEFL), mathematics (SAT-MATH) for non-exempt colleges, and Arabic for the College of Law — on top of UAEU’s general admission requirements.
