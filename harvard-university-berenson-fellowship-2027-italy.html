The Harvard University Berenson Fellowship 2027 offers postdoctoral scholars four or six months of residential research at Villa I Tatti, the Harvard University Center for Italian Renaissance Studies in Florence, Italy. Fellows receive $5,200 a month, a relocation supplement of up to $1,800, and — when available — a rent-free apartment or a housing subsidy, with full access to the Biblioteca Berenson. The fellowship supports research on “Italy in the World”: transnational exchanges between Italy and other cultures during the Renaissance, from the 14th to the 17th century. Applications close on 16 November 2026.

Deadline: 16 Nov Closes 16 Nov Updated 16 Oct 2026 By Villa I Tatti, Harvard University
Award
A grant of $5,200 per month for four or six months, plus a one-time relocation supplement of up to $1,800. When available, fellows may also receive a rent-free apartment (utilities remain the fellow’s responsibility) or, if no apartment can be provided, a housing subsidy. Fellows get full access to the Biblioteca Berenson seven days a week, a personal desk or carrel in the library, weekday lunches with the Villa I Tatti scholarly community, and participation in lectures, conferences and concerts. Up to four fellowships are awarded each year.
Eligible
Scholars of any nationality with a PhD completed at the time of application, researching connections between Italy and other world cultures during the Renaissance (14th–17th centuries). Priority goes to early- and mid-career scholars. Applicants must not have previously held a Term Fellowship at I Tatti (prior Graduate Fellows are excluded from this restriction) and must not be working towards a second PhD at the time of application. Conversational English is required, with familiarity with Italian. There is no age limit and no application fee stated on the official fellowship page.
How
Apply online through the official Villa I Tatti application portal (itatti.slideroom.com), in English or Italian. The application requires a research proposal, a CV, a copy of your PhD certificate, and two academic referees who submit recommendation letters online.
Deadline
16 November 2026, midnight Cambridge, Massachusetts time. The two required recommendation letters must be submitted online by 21 November 2026.
Fine print
The fellowship is residential: the research must be suitable for completion primarily in Florence, and fellows are expected to work only on the project submitted for the fellowship. The four-month option runs September to December; the six-month option runs January to June.

About the fellowship

Villa I Tatti, the Harvard University Center for Italian Renaissance Studies, sits in the hills above Florence in the former home of the art historian Bernard Berenson. The Berenson Fellowship is its flagship residential award for scholars outside Harvard who are working on how Renaissance Italy connected with the rest of the world — the theme the centre calls “Italy in the World”. That covers transnational exchanges between Italy and regions such as Africa, Asia, Latin America and the wider Mediterranean, across architecture, art, history, philosophy, literature, music and the history of science.

The award is deliberately generous in the ways that matter to a researcher: a $5,200 monthly grant, relocation support, a desk in one of the great Renaissance libraries, and daily contact with a small international scholarly community over lunch and in the library. With up to four fellowships a year, it is competitive — but the field is defined by the research theme, not by nationality or discipline.

Eligibility

  • Hold a PhD at the time of application and be able to upload proof of the degree.
  • Propose a research project on “Italy in the World” — transnational exchanges involving Renaissance Italy between the 14th and 17th centuries — that can be completed primarily in Florence.
  • Be open to scholars of all nationalities; early- and mid-career scholars are given priority.
  • Not have previously held a Term Fellowship at I Tatti (prior Graduate Fellows are exempt from this restriction).
  • Not be working towards a second PhD at the time of application.
  • Be conversant in English, with familiarity with Italian; no IELTS score is specified.

Benefits

  • $5,200 per month for four or six months.
  • A one-time relocation supplement of up to $1,800.
  • A rent-free apartment when available (utilities are the fellow’s responsibility), or a housing subsidy if university accommodation is unavailable.
  • Full access to the Biblioteca Berenson seven days a week, with a personal desk or carrel in the library.
  • Weekday lunches with the Villa I Tatti scholarly community and participation in lectures, conferences and concerts.

Documents to prepare

  • A research proposal showing how your project connects Italy with other cultures or regions during the Renaissance.
  • An academic CV.
  • A copy of your PhD certificate.
  • Two academic referees who will submit recommendation letters online (by 21 November 2026).
  • Details of your previous research where the application asks for them.

How to apply

  1. Read the fellowship guidelines and eligibility criteria on the official Villa I Tatti fellowship page.
  2. Prepare your research proposal and CV, and have your PhD certificate ready to upload.
  3. Register two academic referees in the application system so they can submit recommendation letters.
  4. Submit the completed application through the official online portal (itatti.slideroom.com) in English or Italian before 16 November 2026, midnight Cambridge, Massachusetts time.
  5. Make sure your referees submit their letters by 21 November 2026.

FAQs

What research does the Berenson Fellowship support?

Research examining “Italy in the World” between the 14th and 17th centuries — transnational exchanges between Italy and regions such as Africa, Asia, Latin America or the wider Mediterranean, in fields from art history and architecture to literature, music and the history of science.

How long is the fellowship?

Either 4 months (September to December) or 6 months (January to June), residential at Villa I Tatti in Florence.

Do I need to speak Italian?

You need to be conversant in English and have familiarity with Italian; no IELTS score is specified.

Can I apply if I have already held a fellowship at I Tatti?

Not if you have previously held a Term Fellowship at I Tatti. Prior Graduate Fellows are exempt from that restriction.

What is the difference between the application deadline and the recommendation deadline?

Your application must be submitted by 16 November 2026, midnight Cambridge, Massachusetts time. Your two referees have until 21 November 2026 to submit their letters.
