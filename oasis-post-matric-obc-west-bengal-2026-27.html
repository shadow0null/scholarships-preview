About the scholarship

The Oasis Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC Students 2026–27 is West Bengal's dedicated track — run by the Backward Classes Welfare Department — for Other Backward Classes students from Class 11 through postgraduate level. Applications run on the official Oasis portal (oasis.gov.in).

Eligibility

  • Permanent resident (domicile) of West Bengal.
  • Belong to the OBC category with a valid caste certificate.
  • Studying in Class 11 or above — ITI, diploma, degree, PG — at an institution inside India.
  • Annual family income up to Rs 1,00,000 (the OBC-specific limit; SC/ST limit is Rs 2.5 lakh).
  • Passed the previous year's examination.
  • Active bank account in the student's own name, linked with Aadhaar.
  • Not taking any other scholarship.

Benefits

  • Up to Rs 20,000 per year for OBC hostellers (amount varies by course group and level).
  • Maintenance allowance plus reimbursement of compulsory non-refundable fees.
  • Paid by DBT to the Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Documents required

  1. Aadhaar details
  2. OBC caste certificate
  3. Income certificate
  4. Previous exam marksheet
  5. Bank details (Aadhaar-linked)
  6. Photograph + institution details

How to apply

  1. Go to the Oasis portaloasis.gov.in
  2. Register / log inNew applicants register; renewals log in.
  3. Fill the form and upload documents
  4. No closing date announced — applications open
Income limit differs by category: OBC applicants face a Rs 1 lakh ceiling, while SC/ST applicants get Rs 2.5 lakh. Check the correct track before applying.

FAQs

What is the income limit for OBC?

Rs 1,00,000 per year of family income.

How much can I get?

Up to Rs 20,000 per year for hostellers, varying by course group.

Can I apply if I study outside West Bengal?

Yes — the institution must be inside India; West Bengal domicile is what matters.

Where do I apply?

On the official Oasis portal, oasis.gov.in — not on NSP.

Verified 1 Nov 2026 on the live Oasis portal (oasis.wb.gov.in): 2026-27 applications are shown as OPEN for Post-Matric SC, ST and OBC, with no closing date and no closed notice anywhere on the homepage or announcements (announcement: 'Online Application for Scholarship for 2026-27 Session is open for Post matric SC, ST, OBC & Pre Matric Sc, Pre Matric Comp-II, ST, OBC'). The corpus's 31 Oct 2026 date came from a secondary listing and is not stated on the portal, so this article is corrected to 'no closing date announced' rather than flipped to closed.
