About the scholarship

A monthly living allowance for foreign researchers — paid directly, never repaid.

The Honjo International Scholarship Foundation (HISF) funds foreign students pursuing a Master's or doctoral degree at a Japanese graduate school. Around 10 students from all countries are selected each year. You apply directly to the foundation — no university nomination is needed.

Honest note: the scholarship covers the monthly allowance (plus possible conference support). It does not cover university tuition fees or initial travel/airfare to Japan — plan those separately.

How to apply

  1. Prepare your documents

    Transcripts, the Japanese research proposal on the official template, recommendation letter, and admission proof.

  2. Submit the online form

    Complete the application on the HISF website — no physical documents need mailing.

  3. Meet the deadline: 31 Oct 2026

    Document screening results 31 Jan 2027 · interviews 9 Feb 2027 · final results 31 Mar 2027.

Applications go through the official Honjo Foundation portal. It's free — never pay anyone to apply.

FAQs

Does it cover tuition?

No — the scholarship is a monthly living allowance; tuition and airfare are not covered.

Do I need Japanese?

Yes — basic conversational ability. The interview is in Japanese and the research proposal is written in Japanese.

How many are selected?

About 10 students from all countries each year.

Do I need my university to nominate me?

No — you apply directly to the foundation.
