About the scholarships

Yale University in New Haven, Connecticut, is one of the few universities in the world that is need-blind for every applicant, everywhere: a family’s ability to pay is not a factor in the admissions process for any student, anywhere in the world. Once admitted, Yale meets 100% of demonstrated financial need with grants that never have to be repaid. The average need-based scholarship is $75,220 a year, and about 56% of international undergraduates receive Yale grants.

The income markers are worth memorising. Families with typical assets earning under $100,000 normally pay nothing — tuition, fees, housing, food, travel and even hospitalisation insurance are covered, plus a $2,000 start-up grant in the first year. Families earning under $200,000 typically have tuition covered entirely. There are no loans, ever: Yale’s aid package contains zero loan expectation, and around 88% of Yale graduates leave with no student loan debt. For a low- or middle-income family anywhere in the world, Yale can genuinely cost less than a local university. There is no separate scholarship application: financial aid is built into the standard admissions application, and there is no GPA threshold — your grades matter enormously for getting admitted, and admission is the only gate the scholarship sits behind.

Eligibility

  • Be admitted to Yale College (undergraduate) — admission is need-blind worldwide.
  • Be of any nationality — aid meets 100% of demonstrated need regardless of citizenship or immigration status.
  • There is no GPA threshold and no minimum test score for the scholarship itself.
  • Yale offers no merit scholarships — all undergraduate aid is based on financial need.
  • Indicate your interest in financial aid on the admission application; you will then be contacted by Undergraduate Financial Aid with a document list.

Benefits

  • Average need-based scholarship: $75,220 a year.
  • Families under $100,000 (typical assets): $0 — tuition, fees, housing, food, travel and hospitalisation insurance, plus a $2,000 first-year start-up grant.
  • Families under $200,000: tuition typically covered entirely.
  • No loans, ever — aid packages contain zero loan expectation.
  • Supplemental allowances: a $2,000 first-year international allowance and a $1,500 annual vacation allowance for aided internationals unable to return home during breaks (per Collegedunia).

Documents to prepare

  • The admission application (Common Application or Coalition Application) with essay, recommendations and transcripts.
  • The CSS Profile (Yale College code 3987) — required for international students seeking aid.
  • Parent and student tax returns / income documentation (2025 income for the 2027–28 year), submitted via IDOC.
  • US citizens and permanent residents: also the FAFSA (Yale code 001426).
  • Yale does not accept the ISFAA in place of its own forms.
  • English proficiency test (TOEFL/IELTS/Duolingo) where required — if English is not your first language and you have not spent two or more years in an English-medium school.

How to apply

  1. Apply for admission to Yale College: Single-Choice Early Action by 1 November 2026 or Regular Decision by 2 January 2027.
  2. Indicate your interest in financial aid on the admission application.
  3. File the CSS Profile (code 3987) and submit tax documents via IDOC by the priority date: 1 December 2026 (Early Action, international) or 15 February 2027 (Regular Decision).
  4. US citizens and permanent residents also file the FAFSA (code 001426).
  5. Your financial aid offer arrives with your admission decision if your aid application is complete.

FAQs

Is Yale need-blind for international students?

Yes. Yale states that a family’s ability to pay is not a factor in the admissions process for any student, anywhere in the world, and that aid offers meet 100% of demonstrated need regardless of citizenship or immigration status.

How much does Yale cost, and what does the scholarship pay?

The published estimate for 2026–27 is $72,500 tuition plus housing, food, books and personal costs — about $97,985 in total, plus travel. The average need-based scholarship is $75,220 a year; families under $100,000 pay $0 and families under $200,000 pay $0 tuition.

Is there a separate Yale Scholarship application?

No. Financial aid is built directly into the standard admissions application; there is no extra scholarship form to complete. You indicate interest in aid on the application and then file the CSS Profile.

Does applying for aid hurt my admission chances at Yale?

No. Yale is need-blind for every applicant, so financial aid interest never factors into the admissions decision.

What is the Yale application deadline for 2027 entry?

Single-Choice Early Action closes 1 November 2026 and Regular Decision closes 2 January 2027. International aid documents are due 1 December 2026 (Early Action) or 15 February 2027 (Regular Decision).

Does Yale offer merit scholarships?

No. Yale’s financial aid office says merit-based scholarships are not offered by Yale. All undergraduate scholarships are based on financial need.
