About the scholarship

The University of Ottawa Admission Scholarship is an entrance award granted on the strength of the marks used to admit a student. It comes in two forms: a one-time payment for entering undergraduates, scaled to the admission average, and a multi-year funding package for doctoral students. Both are automatic — the university assesses every eligible admission file and informs recipients with their admission offer. The 2026–2027 award details are currently published on the university's official scholarships pages.

It is worth noting that several scholarship-aggregation websites describe this award incorrectly, attaching invented deadlines or mismatched amounts to it. The university itself runs no application round and sets no application deadline for the admission scholarship, so treat any "last date" posted elsewhere with caution and rely on uottawa.ca.

Eligibility

For the undergraduate Admission Scholarship, you must be enrolled full time for the first time in a university undergraduate programme, studying in a direct-entry faculty or in the Faculty of Law, Civil Law Section, with an admission average between 85% and 100%. Mature applicants and university transfers are not eligible for this award. The general undergraduate criteria describe eligibility for Canadian citizens, permanent residents, and those with protected-person or refugee status, with exceptions noted per award.

For the graduate (doctoral) Admission Scholarship, eligible candidates include Canadian students, permanent residents, protected persons, members of a diplomatic corps, and international students admitted into doctoral programmes, with an admission average of 8.0/10 or higher. Certain programmes are excluded from the doctoral award — the university's published list includes the MBA, MHA, MPH, MEd, MEng, Engineering Management, MCom, and MCI.

Benefits

Undergraduate recipients receive a single award of $1,000 (admission average 85–89.9%), $2,000 (90–94.9%), or $3,000 (95–100%). Doctoral recipients receive funding with a minimum total value of $72,000 over four years: the admission-scholarship component pays $9,000 per year in $3,000 instalments per term across 12 consecutive terms of full-time enrolment, with additional funding making up the rest of the package. Doctoral recipients must remain enrolled full time without interruption for the full duration of the scholarship.

Documents required

Not published in the official notification. Because consideration is automatic, there are no scholarship-specific documents to prepare; the university uses the academic records and supporting materials already included in your admission application.

How to apply

  1. Apply for admission. Submit your application for an eligible undergraduate or doctoral programme at the University of Ottawa through the normal admission channel.
  2. Secure your uoAccess ID. Once admitted, you can use your uoAccess ID and password to access university services, including the Online Scholarships and Bursaries tool for other awards that do need applications.
  3. Wait for the offer. No separate scholarship form exists. Eligible students are automatically considered at admission and are informed if they receive the award.
  4. Check the official page. Before relying on any deadline or amount posted on a third-party site, confirm the current terms on the university's official scholarships pages linked below.

FAQs

Is there a separate application for the Admission Scholarship?

No. The University of Ottawa states that no application is required — every eligible admission file is automatically considered, and recipients are informed if they receive the award.

What admission average do I need?

For the undergraduate award: 85–89.9% earns $1,000, 90–94.9% earns $2,000, and 95–100% earns $3,000. For the doctoral award, the published minimum is an admission average of 8.0/10.

Can international students receive it?

International students admitted into doctoral programmes are explicitly listed as eligible for the doctoral admission scholarship (8.0/10 average or higher). For the undergraduate award, the general criteria are framed around Canadian citizens, permanent residents, and protected/refugee status, with exceptions noted per award — international undergraduates have separate dedicated awards at the university.

What is the 30 April 2026 deadline on some websites?

That date appears only on scholarship-aggregation websites and is not published by the University of Ottawa as a deadline for the admission scholarship. Since consideration is automatic with admission, there is no application round to close — be guided by the university’s own admission deadlines instead.

Is the undergraduate award renewable?

Not published in the official notification. The university describes it as a single admission award of $1,000 to $3,000; the doctoral component runs across 12 consecutive terms of full-time enrolment.
