The Oxford Pershing Square Graduate Scholarship 2027 funds up to five exceptional students through the Oxford 1+1 MBA: one year of a Master’s degree, then the one-year Oxford MBA at Saïd Business School, with full course fees for both degrees and a living-cost grant of at least £21,805 for each year of study. The catch is the double admission: you must be admitted to both programmes before you can be considered, and there is no separate scholarship application — your 500-word essay goes in the Funding section of the MBA application. The deadline is 6 January 2027.

Deadline: 6 Jan 2027 Closes 6 Jan 2027 Updated 16 Oct 2026 By University of Oxford
Award
Up to five full awards a year for the Oxford 1+1 MBA. The award covers course fees for both the Master’s degree and the MBA programme, plus a grant for living costs of at least £21,805 for each year of study. Scholars also get mentorship, conferences, termly and scholar community events, and a spring trek to New York hosted by the Pershing Square Foundation. The scholarship is funded in perpetuity by a £13 million endowment from the Pershing Square Foundation and the University of Oxford.
Eligible
All nationalities, with no age limit. You must be admitted both to a 1+1 MBA partnering programme and to the MBA before being considered — applicants on a waiting list are not considered. Priority order: (1) new applicants to the Oxford 1+1 MBA; (2) applicants admitted to both a full-time one-year Master’s course not on the partnering list and the MBA; (3) current Master’s students admitted to the MBA who were not considered in the previous year. You need the intention to focus on addressing world-scale social challenges, leadership potential, academic achievement (including degree results and GMAT or GRE score), and strong personal character and integrity.
How
Apply to both programmes in the same application cycle: the Master’s programme and the Oxford MBA. Upload the scholarship essay (maximum 500 words, answering "How do you intend to change the world, and what does this tell us about you as a person?") in the Funding section of the 1+1 MBA application. There is no separate scholarship application.
Deadline
6 January 2027 for the 1+1 MBA application. Your chosen Master’s programme has its own application deadline in December 2026 or January 2027, which you must also meet. One tracker gives 7 January 2027 and another 8 January 2027; Oxford’s published deadline is 6 January 2027, which is used here.
Fine print
Admission to both programmes is a hard requirement — being on a waiting list for either one disqualifies you. Shortlisting takes place in early April 2027, with interviews in late April or early May. With only five awards a year and the double-admission requirement, this is one of the most competitive scholarships on this site.

About the scholarship

The Oxford-Pershing Square Graduate Scholarships are funded by the Pershing Square Foundation — established by Bill Ackman and Karen Ackman — in partnership with the University of Oxford. The Foundation’s gift of £7.8 million, combined with £5.2 million from the University’s Graduate Scholarship Matched Fund, created a £13 million endowment, so the scholarship is funded in perpetuity rather than year to year.

What makes this award different from most Oxford MBA scholarships is its scope. It does not fund the MBA year alone — it covers both degrees in the Oxford 1+1 MBA: a one-year Master’s degree in a specialist subject, then the one-year MBA at Saïd Business School. That is two full Oxford degrees, both fully funded, with living costs for each year of study, plus mentorship, conferences and a spring trek to New York. The price is the double admission: you must win a place on both programmes in the same cycle, and only then are you considered for one of the five awards.

Eligibility

  • Be of any nationality; there is no age limit.
  • Be admitted to both a 1+1 MBA partnering programme and the MBA before being considered — applicants on a waiting list are not considered.
  • Priority is given to new applicants to the Oxford 1+1 MBA; then to applicants admitted to a full-time one-year Master’s course not on the partnering list plus the MBA; then to current Master’s students admitted to the MBA who were not considered in the previous year.
  • Show the intention to focus on addressing world-scale social challenges in your career, in an existing organisation or through a new enterprise.
  • Demonstrate leadership potential and academic achievement, including degree results and GMAT or GRE score.
  • Show strong personal character, integrity and commitment, and a clear vision of how the 1+1 MBA will help you achieve your goals.

Benefits

  • Course fees for both degrees — the Master’s degree and the MBA.
  • A living-cost grant of at least £21,805 for each year of study.
  • Mentorship, conferences, termly and scholar community events.
  • A spring trek to New York hosted by the Pershing Square Foundation.
  • Two full Oxford degrees under one funding umbrella — the only award that funds the complete 1+1 MBA combination.

Documents to prepare

  • Academic transcripts and degree certificate.
  • An updated CV.
  • A statement of purpose.
  • The scholarship essay (maximum 500 words): "How do you intend to change the world, and what does this tell us about you as a person?"
  • English-language evidence if your previous education was not in English.
  • GMAT or GRE scores and letters of recommendation, as the MBA and Master’s applications require.
  • A valid passport or identification document.

How to apply

  1. Choose an eligible Master’s programme that participates in the Oxford 1+1 MBA Programme.
  2. Submit separate applications for the Master’s programme and the Oxford MBA, in the same application cycle.
  3. Upload the scholarship essay (maximum 500 words) in the Funding section of the 1+1 MBA application.
  4. Make sure both applications are complete and submitted by their respective deadlines — the MBA by 6 January 2027, the Master’s by its own deadline in December 2026 or January 2027.
  5. There is no separate scholarship application — consideration is based on the two programme applications and the essay.

FAQs

Is the Oxford Pershing Square Scholarship fully funded?

Yes. It covers full course fees for both the Master’s degree and the MBA, plus a living-cost grant of at least £21,805 for each year of study.

How many scholarships are awarded each year?

Up to five scholarships are awarded annually.

What is the deadline for the Oxford Pershing Square Scholarship 2027?

6 January 2027 for the 1+1 MBA application. Your chosen Master’s programme has its own deadline in December 2026 or January 2027. One tracker gives 7 January and another 8 January 2027; Oxford’s published deadline of 6 January 2027 is used here.

Do I need to apply separately for the scholarship?

No. There is no separate scholarship application. You apply to both programmes and upload the 500-word scholarship essay in the Funding section of the 1+1 MBA application.

Can I apply if I am already a Master’s student at Oxford?

Current Master’s students admitted to the MBA who were not considered for the scholarship in the previous year are considered, but at the lowest priority. New applicants to the 1+1 MBA are considered first.

What is the scholarship essay question?

"How do you intend to change the world, and what does this tell us about you as a person?" — in 500 words or fewer.
