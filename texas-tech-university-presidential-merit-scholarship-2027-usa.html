About the scholarship

Texas Tech University in Lubbock, Texas, awards Presidential Merit Scholarships to entering first-time freshmen who show exceptional academic ability. The award is not a single fixed sum and it is not a separate competition: it is the university’s automatic merit assessment, made on the strength of your admission application. The official page states the rule plainly — merit awards are guaranteed to freshmen admitted by 1 April for fall entry, and transfer students may qualify if admitted by 31 July for fall entry or 5 January for spring entry. Admission after those dates is limited only by the availability of the scholarships.

Two corrections to the aggregator version of this award matter. First, the widely repeated “$26,000” is an aggregator total, not a figure on the university’s tier table, and this article does not present it as the official award. Second, some aggregator pages describe the award as open to United States residents only, while the university’s own page says a non-resident awarded a Presidential Scholarship also qualifies to pay in-state tuition — so non-residents are plainly in scope, and the in-state rate is a major part of the value for them.

Benefits

  • Annual award: $1,000–$9,000 a year, set by the combination of test score and unweighted high-school GPA. At the top of the table, a 1500–1600 SAT (or 34–36 ACT) with a 4.0–3.96 GPA earns $9,000 a year; a 1100–1190 SAT (or 22–24 ACT) with a 3.49–3.0 GPA earns $1,000 a year. Test-optional applicants can add $1,000 if they rank in the top 10% of their class.
  • How it is paid: one half toward the fall term and one half toward the spring term, renewable annually for up to four years subject to the university’s renewal terms.
  • In-state tuition for non-residents: if you are a non-resident or international student awarded a Presidential Scholarship, you also qualify to pay in-state tuition — a saving that dwarfs the cash award.
  • National Merit Finalists: a separate line on the official page covers 100% of the originally federally approved cost of attendance for National Merit Finalists.

Who can apply

  • Entering first-time freshmen who show exceptional academic ability through superior test scores and class ranking. Transfer students are also considered for merit awards under their own admission dates.
  • Test score or GPA route: the published routes are a minimum cumulative SAT of 1100 or a minimum composite ACT of 22 with a minimum high-school GPA of 3.0, or a minimum high-school GPA of 3.5 without test scores.
  • Full-time enrolment and progress: renewal requires satisfactory academic progress under the university’s terms, including the credit-hour expectations that apply to merit awards.
  • No separate application: there is no scholarship form beyond the admission application itself — apply for admission and you are considered automatically.

Documents required

  • The admission application itself — the official page names no separate scholarship document set, because consideration is automatic on admission.
  • Academic records and test scores as required for admission (transcripts, SAT/ACT scores where submitted, class rank where available).
  • International applicants should confirm English-proficiency and credential requirements on the university’s international admissions pages, which are separate from the scholarship table.

How to apply

  1. Apply for admission to Texas Tech University as an entering first-time freshman (or as a transfer student under the transfer dates), through the university’s admission route.
  2. Make sure your admission application is complete by 1 April 2027 — merit awards are guaranteed to freshmen admitted by this date for fall entry. A 1 December 2026 fall priority scholarship date is also listed on the university’s pages, so applying earlier is safer.
  3. There is no separate scholarship application to submit. Your award, if any, is determined by the tier table from your test score and GPA and is communicated with your admission outcome.
  4. If you are a non-resident recipient, confirm the in-state tuition qualification with the scholarships office when you accept, since that is where most of the value sits for out-of-state students.

FAQs

What is the deadline for the Texas Tech Presidential Merit Scholarship 2027?

Merit awards are guaranteed to freshmen admitted by 1 April 2027 for fall entry. A 1 December 2026 fall priority scholarship date is also listed on the university’s pages, so do not leave the application to the last day. Transfer students have their own dates: 31 July for fall entry and 5 January for spring entry.

How much is the Presidential Merit Scholarship worth?

Between $1,000 and $9,000 a year, depending on your test score and unweighted high-school GPA under the university’s published tier table — not a single flat amount. The “$26,000” total repeated by aggregator pages is not the university’s published award and should not be relied on.

Do international students qualify?

The university’s own page says a non-resident awarded a Presidential Scholarship also qualifies to pay in-state tuition, so non-residents are in scope. Some aggregator pages wrongly describe the award as United States residents only; follow the university page, and confirm your own fee status with the scholarships office.

Is there a separate scholarship application?

No. There is no scholarship form beyond the admission application. You are automatically considered when you apply for admission, and the award is set by the tier table.

What happens if I apply after 1 April?

Admission after the guarantee date is limited only by the availability of the scholarships — awards are no longer guaranteed. Apply early to keep the guarantee.

What does the award cover, and for how long?

The cash award is paid half in fall and half in spring and is renewable annually for up to four years subject to the university’s renewal terms. For non-residents, the accompanying in-state tuition qualification is usually worth more than the cash award.
