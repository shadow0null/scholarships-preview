About the scholarship

Trent University International Scholarships 2027 support international students starting an undergraduate degree at Trent University in Peterborough, Ontario. Every admitted international student is automatically considered for an International Entrance Scholarship based on grades, while high-achieving students can apply separately for competitive International Prestigious Scholarships & Awards worth up to CAD 15,000 a year.

Partial funding: these are tuition and ancillary-fee awards, not full cost-of-living packages. Trent pairs entrance and prestigious awards — you can hold both — but plan for living costs separately.

Eligibility

  • An international student paying international tuition fees, studying in Canada on a study permit.
  • Applied to an undergraduate degree at Trent — postgraduate students are not eligible for these awards.
  • A Canadian-equivalent high-school average of 80% or higher.
  • Meet Trent’s admission and English-language proficiency requirements.
  • Fulfil your offer conditions, register for courses and pay your first-year fees.
  • Prestigious awards additionally require you to start in the spring, summer or fall term — Winter (January) starters are not eligible for the prestigious tier.

Benefits

  • International Entrance Scholarship (automatic): CAD 2,500–5,500 per academic year by average — $5,500 for 95–100%, $4,500 for 90–94.9%, $3,500 for 85–89.9%, $2,500 for 80–84.9%. Renewable each year (reapply and maintain 80%+ on a full course load), worth up to CAD 22,000 over four years.
  • International Prestigious Scholarships & Awards (application required): up to CAD 15,000 per year, up to CAD 60,000 over four years — competitive, based on academics, community service and leadership, with some consideration of financial need.
  • Moore Family International Scholarship: up to CAD 12,000 per year, up to CAD 48,000 over four years — for students from outside North America.
  • Justin Chiu International Scholarship: up to CAD 15,000 per year, up to CAD 60,000 over four years — for international students from Asia.
  • Daniel Powell Scholarship: renewable award for Latin American students entering Trent, intended for Global Development Studies majors (amount not published on the awards page).
  • You can combine entrance and prestigious awards.

Documents required

Trent’s official scholarships page does not publish a separate documents checklist — the prestigious-award application is completed inside the Trent Applicant Portal (Finances tab > International), which will show the fields it needs. Your admission application file (transcripts, English proficiency) forms the basis of the automatic entrance award.

How to apply

  1. Apply for admission to TrentYou are automatically considered for the International Entrance Scholarship — no separate form.
  2. Open the prestigious application from 1 NovemberPrestigious scholarship applications open each year on 1 November inside the Trent Applicant Portal (Finances tab > International). One application covers all the prestigious awards you are eligible for.
  3. Submit by 15 February 2027, 11:59 p.m. ESTThe deadline for International Prestigious Scholarships applications.
  4. Fulfil your offer conditionsRegister for courses and pay first-year fees to secure your awards.

Questions: international@trentu.ca.

FAQs

Is the Trent entrance scholarship automatic?

Yes — every international undergraduate applicant is automatically considered for the International Entrance Scholarship based on their admission average. No separate form is needed.

What is the deadline for Trent’s prestigious scholarships?

15 February 2027 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. Applications open on 1 November 2026.

How much can I get?

Entrance awards run CAD 2,500–5,500 a year (renewable); competitive prestigious awards go up to CAD 15,000 a year. The two can be combined.

Are January (winter) starters eligible?

They can receive automatic entrance awards, but they are not eligible for the International Prestigious Scholarships.

Are postgraduate students eligible?

No — these international awards are for undergraduate degrees only.
