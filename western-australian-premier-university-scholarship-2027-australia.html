About the scholarship

The Western Australian Premier’s University Scholarship is the Government of Western Australia’s flagship award for attracting high-achieving international students to the state’s universities. At the University of Western Australia (UWA) in Perth, it takes the form of a one-time AUD $50,000 grant applied directly to tuition fees for a commencing postgraduate student.

UWA’s official listing currently shows the status as “Opening Soon”: applications open 16 November 2026 and close 19 March 2027 (3:00pm), with outcomes notified in May 2027. An honest note first: $50,000 is a serious contribution, but it is not a full ride — plan for remaining tuition, accommodation, food, transport, health insurance, visa costs and flights.

Eligibility

  • Have applied and received an offer from UWA for a postgraduate course as an onshore international student, studying full time for at least two years.
  • Be commencing studies onshore for Semester 1, 2027.
  • Meet the criteria for an Australian international student visa.
  • Not have commenced or completed previous studies in Western Australia, and not be entering on a packaged, foundation or English-language pathway.
  • Be a citizen of one of the 21 listed countries, including India, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines, Vietnam, Kenya, China, Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Brazil, Colombia and the UK.
  • Excluded at UWA: one-year law courses, 1.5-year arts courses, and business/science courses carrying 24 points of admission credit (these finish in 1.5 years).

Benefits

  • A one-time AUD $50,000 payment applied directly against tuition fees.
  • The prestige of a government-funded flagship award, nominated through the university.
  • Study at UWA in Perth, one of Australia’s leading research universities.

Documents required

  • The completed UWA scholarship application form (opens 16 November 2026).
  • A written statement of 500–1000 words outlining why the scholarship would benefit you and how a UWA postgraduate degree fits into your future plans.
  • Evidence of community service.
  • Evidence of recognition awarded for academic, community or sporting achievement.
  • Your UWA offer for the postgraduate course (you must hold the offer to apply).

FAQs

Is the WA Premier’s Scholarship fully funded?

No. It is a one-time AUD $50,000 grant applied to tuition — a major contribution, but you must budget separately for remaining tuition, living costs, insurance, visa and flights. That is why this article is labelled Funded, not Fully Funded.

I am offshore. Can I apply at UWA?

UWA’s instance requires onshore commencement for Semester 1, 2027. The other four WA universities run their own Premier’s scholarships with their own terms — check each university’s page.

Is it for undergraduates too?

The WA Government program generally covers undergraduate and postgraduate study, but UWA’s instance in this round is postgraduate. Check Curtin, Murdoch, ECU and Notre Dame for their own eligibility.

Do I need a separate application?

Yes — unlike UWA’s automatic Global Excellence Scholarship, the Premier’s Scholarship needs its own application with the written statement and evidence.

When will I hear back?

UWA notifies applicants of the outcome in May 2027.

Which countries are eligible?

21 listed countries at UWA, including India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines, Vietnam, Kenya, China, Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Brazil, Colombia and the UK.
