About the scholarship

The Honjo International Scholarship Foundation (HISF) funds foreign students pursuing a Master's or doctoral degree at a Japanese graduate school. Around 10 students from all countries are selected each year. You apply directly to the foundation — no university nomination is needed. The award is a monthly allowance you do not repay.

Honest note: the scholarship covers the monthly allowance (plus possible conference support). It does not cover university tuition fees or initial travel/airfare to Japan — plan those separately.

Eligibility

  • Not a Japanese citizen; any nationality and any research field may apply.
  • Enrolled in, or applying to, a Master's or doctoral programme at a Japanese graduate school (admission or acceptance letter needed).
  • Start the Master's course by age 30, or the PhD course by age 35 — the foundation states no exceptions.
  • More than 12 months remaining until graduation.
  • Basic conversational Japanese — the interview is conducted in Japanese.
  • Strong commitment to serving your home country in your future career.
  • Professional graduate school students are generally ineligible unless submitting a research plan.

Benefits

  • ¥230,000/month for 1–2 year degrees.
  • ¥210,000/month for 3-year degrees.
  • ¥180,000/month for 4–5 year degrees.
  • Paid for the minimum period required to complete the degree.
  • Possible financial assistance for international conference attendance under the foundation's rules.

Documents required

  1. Academic transcripts (undergraduate + graduate if applicable; in Japanese or English)
  2. Research proposal on the official template, written in Japanese
  3. Recommendation letter from current/previous academic supervisor
  4. Proof of admission / certificate of enrollment

How to apply

  1. Prepare documentsTranscripts, the Japanese research proposal template, recommendation letter and admission proof.
  2. Submit onlineComplete the application form on the HISF website — no physical documents need mailing.
  3. Deadline: 31 Oct 2026Document screening results 31 Jan 2027; interviews 9 Feb 2027; final results 31 Mar 2027.

Applications go through the official Honjo Foundation portal. It's free — never pay anyone to apply.

FAQs

Does it cover tuition?

No — the scholarship is a monthly living allowance; tuition and airfare are not covered.

Do I need Japanese?

Yes — basic conversational ability; the interview is in Japanese and the research proposal is written in Japanese.

How many are selected?

About 10 students from all countries each year.

Do I need my university to nominate me?

No — you apply directly to the foundation.
