About the scholarship

CEEPUS — the Central European Exchange Program for University Studies — is a regional exchange scheme run by 16 member countries in Central Europe and the Western Balkans. It funds students and teachers to spend time at a partner university in another member country: 3–5 months for regular student mobility, 1–2 months for short-term thesis or research mobility, and from 5 working days for teachers. Hungary is one of the most popular host countries in the network, and this article covers the Hungarian route.

The funding is a monthly stipend — HUF 170,000 a month for students in Hungary, according to most sources — plus a full tuition-fee exemption at the host university. One Hungarian university page lists HUF 190,000 a month for students instead, so the exact figure should be confirmed on ceepus.info before you budget. Travel and housing are the grey areas: aggregators report that travel is reimbursed or covered by your home country’s National CEEPUS Office and that dormitory placement or a housing allowance is available, but neither is confirmed on an official page retrieved — treat them as reported, not guaranteed. And be clear about what this is: an exchange-mobility grant, not a full degree scholarship. You stay enrolled at your home university and earn credits abroad.

Eligibility

  • Be enrolled or employed at an accredited institution in a CEEPUS member country: Albania, Austria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, the Czech Republic, Hungary, Kosovo, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Poland, Romania, Serbia, Slovakia and Slovenia.
  • Students must have completed at least two semesters of studies.
  • Short-term student mobility is for final-year Bachelor’s/Master’s students working on a thesis and PhD students.
  • Do not apply for a scholarship in your home country (citizenship or residence).
  • No double funding for the same mobility.
  • Citizens of member countries studying in non-member countries are not eligible.
  • Freemover applicants (whose home university is not in a CEEPUS network) need a Letter of Acceptance from the Hungarian host university.

Benefits

  • A monthly stipend of HUF 170,000 for students in Hungary (most sources; one Hungarian university page lists HUF 190,000 — confirm on ceepus.info).
  • Full tuition-fee exemption at the host institution.
  • Transferable academic credits towards your degree.
  • Support for thesis research, laboratory work and internships during the stay.
  • Travel costs reported as reimbursed or covered by the home country’s National CEEPUS Office — reported by aggregators, not confirmed officially.
  • Dormitory placement or a housing allowance reported by aggregators — not confirmed officially.

Documents to prepare

  • The completed online application form on ceepus.info.
  • A motivation letter outlining your study or research plans.
  • A Letter of Acceptance from the Hungarian host university (Freemover applicants only).
  • Academic transcripts (for students).
  • Proof of enrolment or employment at your home institution.
  • A CV (recommended).
  • A language certificate if applicable.

How to apply

  1. Go to the official website, ceepus.info, and register / log in under “My CEEPUS”.
  2. Use the Mobility Wizard to guide your application.
  3. Select a host institution — through a CEEPUS network, or as a Freemover with a Letter of Acceptance.
  4. Upload the required documents (motivation letter, transcripts, proof of enrolment/employment, CV, language certificate if applicable).
  5. Submit before the deadline for your mobility type and semester.

FAQs

What is the deadline for the CEEPUS Scholarship 2027 in Hungary?

For the summer semester: Network Mobility closes 31 December 2026 and Freemover closes 30 November 2026. The winter-semester deadlines (31 August 2026 for Network Mobility) have passed. Deadlines repeat annually on the same dates.

How much is the CEEPUS stipend in Hungary?

HUF 170,000 a month for students, according to most sources — but one Hungarian university page lists HUF 190,000 a month for students. The exact figure should be confirmed on ceepus.info before you budget.

Is CEEPUS fully funded?

In the aggregator sense only. It pays a monthly stipend and exempts you from tuition at the host university for an exchange stay of 1–10 months. It is a mobility grant, not a full degree scholarship, and travel and housing coverage is reported rather than guaranteed.

Who can apply for CEEPUS?

Students and teachers enrolled or employed at an accredited institution in one of the 16 CEEPUS member countries, who have completed at least two semesters (students). You cannot apply in your home country, cannot receive double funding for the same mobility, and citizens of member countries studying in non-member countries are not eligible.

What is a Freemover application?

An application for students whose home university is not part of a CEEPUS network. Freemovers need a Letter of Acceptance from the Hungarian host university and apply through the same ceepus.info portal.

Which countries are CEEPUS members?

Albania, Austria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, the Czech Republic, Hungary, Kosovo, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Poland, Romania, Serbia, Slovakia and Slovenia.
