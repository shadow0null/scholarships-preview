About the programme

The British Academy Elevate Programme is the Academy’s flagship development offer for early-career researchers in SHAPE — the Social Sciences, Humanities and the Arts for People and the Economy. The Leadership Stream is its leadership-development strand: a structured programme that builds the skills, networks and confidence researchers need to lead in academic and public life, from running research teams and influencing policy to shaping the future of their disciplines.

Elevate runs as a three-year pilot backed by a £5 million investment from Wellcome, and participation is free of charge — participants do not pay to take part. Each year a selective cohort of 62 participants is drawn from members of the British Academy Early Career Researcher Network (ECRN) and Wellcome-funded researchers, and the programme is designed to work across early, mid and late early-career stages. Delivery mixes roughly ten in-person sessions of one to two days at locations across the UK (for UK-based participants, who are expected to travel) with online provision for those based outside the UK.

Eligibility

  • Be an early-career researcher in a SHAPE discipline — Social Sciences, Humanities and the Arts for People and the Economy.
  • Be a member of the British Academy Early Career Researcher Network (ECRN) or a Wellcome-funded researcher.
  • Fit anywhere in the early, mid or late early-career stage — the programme adapts to the stage you are at.
  • If based in the UK, be ready to attend about ten in-person sessions of one to two days each in different UK locations.
  • If based outside the UK, join via the online delivery route.

Benefits

  • Free of charge — all courses are free; you do not pay to participate.
  • A structured leadership-development journey designed for SHAPE researchers, spanning early, mid and late early-career stages.
  • A national cohort of 62 peers — a built-in network across the humanities and social sciences.
  • In-person sessions across the UK (for UK-based participants) with online delivery for those based elsewhere.
  • Post-programme support: within nine months of completing, you organise a Network event funded by the British Academy and mentor the next cohort — leadership practice, not just leadership theory.

FAQs

Is Elevate a cash award?

No. Elevate is a funded professional-development programme, not a grant or prize. Participation is free of charge — the funding (a £5 million Wellcome investment in the three-year pilot) goes into running the programme, not into payments to participants.

Who can apply?

Early-career researchers in SHAPE disciplines — Social Sciences, Humanities and the Arts for People and the Economy — who are members of the British Academy Early Career Researcher Network or Wellcome-funded researchers. The programme works across early, mid and late early-career stages.

Can I take part from outside the UK?

Yes. UK-based participants attend about ten in-person sessions around the country, but online delivery is available specifically for participants based outside the UK.

What happens after the programme ends?

Within nine months of completing, participants organise an event for the Network — funded by the British Academy — and mentor the next cohort. It is a built-in leadership exercise, not optional extras.

When does the 2027 cohort run?

The “2027” cohort runs from March to June 2027. Applications for it are made in autumn 2026, closing 28 October 2026.
