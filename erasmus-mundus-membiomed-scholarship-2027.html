About the scholarship

MemBioMed is an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master — officially titled “From Membrane Biology to Neuroscience and Experimental Medicine” — co-funded by the European Union. Three partner universities drive the programme: Université Côte d’Azur in France (the coordinator), the University of Genoa in Italy, and Osnabrück University in Germany. Each cohort has 15 Erasmus Mundus scholarships, each worth €1,400 per month for 24 months plus full tuition. Applications for the 2027–2029 cohort open on 1 December 2026.

Not “biomedical engineering”: many third-party blogs mislabel this as a biomedical engineering scholarship. The official programme is a neuroscience and experimental-medicine master’s built around biomembranes — apply only if that is the field you actually want to study. Also note the 2026–2028 cohort is closed; this article is written for the newly announced 2027–2029 cycle.

Eligibility

  • All nationalities may apply.
  • A bachelor’s degree worth 180 ECTS in biology, life sciences or a related field.
  • English at B2 level or above (e.g. IELTS 6.5, TOEFL iBT 93 or equivalent).
Closing date not yet published: the official consortium page confirms the 1 December 2026 opening but has not announced the 2027–2029 application deadline. Do not trust a closing date from a third-party site — watch the official page and prepare your documents now so you can apply the week it opens.

Benefits

  • €1,400 per month for 24 months (€33,600 total living support).
  • Full tuition coverage.
  • Study mobility across three European countries (France, Italy, Germany).

Documents required

Per the official consortium guidance: the online application form; a Europass CV; a motivation letter; transcripts and your bachelor’s diploma with an English translation; a course list with grades; two recommendation letters; proof of English proficiency; and a passport or ID copy. Apply through the consortium’s e-candidat portal.

FAQs

When do MemBioMed 2027 applications open?

1 December 2026, for the 2027–2029 cohort. The closing date has not been announced yet on the official page.

What does the MemBioMed scholarship cover?

€1,400 per month for 24 months (€33,600) plus full tuition — 15 Erasmus Mundus scholarships are available per cohort.

What is MemBioMed, exactly?

An Erasmus Mundus Joint Master titled “From Membrane Biology to Neuroscience and Experimental Medicine” — a two-year neuroscience master’s run by Université Côte d’Azur (coordinator), the University of Genoa and Osnabrück University. It is not a biomedical engineering programme.

Who can apply?

Students of all nationalities with a 180-ECTS bachelor’s in biology, life sciences or a related field, and English at B2 level or above.

How do I apply?

Through the consortium’s e-candidat portal (linked from the official MemBioMed page at life.univ-cotedazur.eu), with a Europass CV, motivation letter, transcripts and diploma with English translations, course list with grades, two recommendation letters, English proof and passport/ID.
