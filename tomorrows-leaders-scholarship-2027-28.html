About the scholarship

The Tomorrow’s Leaders Program, funded by the U.S. Department of State’s Middle East Partnership Initiative (MEPI) and administered by FHI 360, offers fully funded undergraduate degrees to outstanding students from the Middle East and North Africa. Scholars study at two US-accredited universities in Lebanon — the American University of Beirut (AUB) and the Lebanese American University (LAU) — with the opportunity to spend one semester in the United States.

The package is genuinely comprehensive: tuition and enrollment fees (up to four academic years), health insurance, housing, visa and residency fees, textbooks and a laptop, two round trips home per year, a monthly living stipend during programme activities, internships, faculty mentorship and leadership workshops. Applications for the 2027–2028 intake opened in October 2026.

Eligibility

  • Citizen of Algeria, Egypt, Iraq, Jordan, Lebanon, Libya, Morocco, Syria, Tunisia, Yemen or the West Bank — Palestinian students residing in a third country with legal residency may also apply.
  • US citizens and US permanent residents are NOT eligible.
  • Current high-school seniors or 2026 graduates (2025 graduates are additionally eligible for Iraq, Libya, Syria, Yemen, the West Bank and third-country Palestinians).
  • Strong academics, maturity, flexibility and leadership potential.
  • English proficiency required — classes are taught in English; a free ASU online assessment is part of the application, and TOEFL/IELTS scores are required only for shortlisted finalists.
  • Must be able to begin undergraduate studies in August 2027.

Benefits

  • Full tuition and enrollment fees for up to four academic years.
  • Housing, health insurance and visa/residency fees covered.
  • Textbooks and a laptop; two round trips home per year; monthly living stipend during programme activities.
  • Internships, faculty mentorship and leadership workshops — plus the chance to spend one semester studying in the US.

Documents required

  • School transcripts (grades 10–12) and, if graduated, the graduation certificate/diploma (with English translations where needed).
  • Evidence of extracurricular and volunteer activities.
  • Two recommendation letters (at least one academic).
  • The online English assessment taken as part of the application.

How to apply

FAQs

Where do scholars actually study?

At the American University of Beirut (AUB) or the Lebanese American University (LAU) in Lebanon — the programme funds full degrees there, with one optional semester of study in the US.

Can US citizens apply?

No — US citizens and US permanent residents are explicitly excluded.

Is there a graduate (master’s) component?

The 2027–2028 call being advertised is the undergraduate programme. A graduate track has existed at LAU, but it is not the intake currently advertised — the details here cover the undergraduate track.

Do I need TOEFL or IELTS before applying?

No — the application includes a free online English assessment; TOEFL or IELTS scores are required only for shortlisted finalists.

Can Gaza residents apply?

Some trackers report that Gaza residents cannot apply for this intake because of travel-access limitations, but this is not confirmed on the official programme site — check with the programme directly if this affects you.
