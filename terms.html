Terms of Use

StudyDesk Scholarships is a free listing guide. These terms state plainly what this site is, what it does, and what it does not do.

What this site is

A guide to scholarships, fellowships and internships for Indian students and students applying abroad. Every listing is checked against the provider's own notification, portal or press release before publication, and each page shows the date it was checked. There is no account, no sign-in, and no profile on this site.

Free to use

There is nothing to pay for here. We never ask for a fee, and no one acting for us will ask for one either. Saving a scholarship to My plan stores it in your browser on your own device; nothing is uploaded.

Applications happen elsewhere

Applications always happen on the provider's own website or portal — never on StudyDesk Scholarships. We do not collect application forms, documents, or personal details for any scholarship. A link is called “Official source” only when it goes to the provider or a government portal; aggregator pages are labelled “Mentioned by”, never “Official source”.

Accuracy and no guarantees

Deadlines, amounts and eligibility change. We re-check listings against official sources, and deadlines on this site are recomputed daily from each listing's own date — but we cannot guarantee that a listing is current at the moment you read it. Always confirm the deadline, amount and eligibility on the official portal before applying. If you spot an error, tell us at scholarships@studydesk.fun and we will check it against the official source.

Advertising

Ads may be served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads on this site. We do not control Google's cookies; you can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings and by clearing cookies in your browser.

Not a government website

StudyDesk is an independent education guide — not a government website and not affiliated with any provider listed here. Always confirm details on the official portal before applying.
