About the scholarship

The Muskaan Scholarship Program 2.0 (2026–27) is a CSR initiative of Valvoline Cummins Private Limited (VCPL), implemented through Buddy4Study. It helps schoolchildren from eligible family backgrounds continue their studies by reducing the financial burden of education.

Alongside money, the programme provides mentorship support, including a session on road safety.

Eligibility

  • Studying in Class 9, 10, 11 or 12 in 2026–27, in one of the 18 eligible states/UTs: Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Tripura, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana and West Bengal.
  • Belong to one of: children of commercial drivers (LMV/HMV licence holders), children of mechanics, or EWS category students.
  • Minimum 60% marks in the previous class.
  • Total annual family income not exceeding Rs 8 lakh from all sources.
  • Children of VCPL or Buddy4Study employees are not eligible.

Benefits

  • Financial grant of up to Rs 12,000, disbursed based on actual verified educational expenses (tuition, books, uniforms, stationery — keep every receipt).
  • Mentorship support, including a dedicated road-safety session.
Reimbursement-based: if your documented expenses are less than Rs 12,000, you receive only the receipt-backed amount. Collect fee receipts, book bills and uniform receipts through the year.

Documents required

As listed on the official application form. Keep your previous class marksheet, ID proof, parent's occupation proof (driving licence / mechanic declaration), income proof, expense receipts and bank details ready.

How to apply

  1. Open the programme pageOn the Buddy4Study portal.
  2. Check state + category eligibilityConfirm your state is among the 18 and your category fits.
  3. Fill and uploadPersonal, academic, occupation and income details with documents.
  4. Applications closed 30 October 2026Online only.

FAQs

My father is an auto-rickshaw driver. Are we eligible?

The programme specifies commercial LMV/HMV licence holders — check the official page for how your licence category is treated.

Is it only for drivers' children?

No — children of mechanics and EWS-category students are also eligible.

Which states are covered?

18 states/UTs across southern, eastern and northeastern India (list in Eligibility above).

How is the Rs 12,000 paid?

Based on actual verified expenses via direct bank transfer — keep all receipts.
